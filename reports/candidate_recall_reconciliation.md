# Forensic Candidate Recall Reconciliation Report

## 1. Executive Summary

A forensic audit was conducted to resolve the apparent discrepancy between the **85.62%** candidate recall reported in early blocking research and the **75.74%** candidate recall measured during the deep diagnostic on the frozen 5,000-entity validation split.

### The Root Cause of the Discrepancy:
The two numbers represent **two entirely different candidate generation architectures evaluated on two different sample populations**:

1. **The 85.62% figure** was produced by `src/blocking_research_v2.py` during Phase 6 exploratory research on all **20,000 entities** (69,115 ground truth matches) using an **unconstrained 7-channel experimental blocking engine** (Base + Token_Num + Compact + Token_Addr + Rare_Stem) that generated **951.35 candidates per S1 entity** (over 19 million candidate pairs).
2. **The 75.74% figure** was produced by `src/run_ablations_and_adversarial.py` and `src/optimize_decision_engine.py` on the **exact 5,000 validation entities** (17,164 ground truth matches) using the **lean, memory-efficient 2-channel blocking engine** (Legal-Stripped Name + Token_Num) that was coupled with the LightGBM classifier to produce the frozen **Macro F0.5 = 0.81172**.

## 3. Forensic Checklist & Point-by-Point Reconciliation

1. **Exact Source of 85.62%**: `reports/blocking_analysis.md` (lines 43-47) generated by `src/blocking_research_v2.py` (lines 243-256).
2. **Exact Source of 75.74%**: `reports/DIAGNOSTIC_FINAL_REPORT.md` (lines 42-45) generated by `src/run_diagnostic_analysis.py` mirroring `src/run_ablations_and_adversarial.py`.
3. **Validation Entity List**: 85.62% used all 20,000 entities from `reports/val_s1_ids.txt`. 75.74% used the standard 5,000 entity subset (`val_s1_ids[:5000]`).
4. **Ground-Truth Population**: 85.62% evaluated on 69,115 true matches. 75.74% evaluated on 17,164 true matches.
5. **Blocking Implementation / Version**: 85.62% used the 7-channel exploratory v2 blocking. 75.74% used the deployed 2-channel blocking (Legal-Stripped + Token_Num).
6. **Metric Definition**: Both calculated pairwise micro-recall (`sum(surfaced) / sum(total_gt)`). On the 5,000 entity set, entity-level macro candidate recall is **78.41%**.
7. **Source 2 / Source 3 Filtering**: Identical in both (all records across `train_source2.tsv` and `train_source3.tsv` were scanned).
8. **Duplicate Handling**: Identical in both (sets of candidate IDs per S1 entity).
9. **Multi-Match Handling**: Identical in both (all ground truth links per entity tracked in sets).
10. **Candidate Capping**: The 85.62% research benchmark had NO candidate cap (averaged 951 cands/S1). In contrast, production inference (`src/inference_partitioned.py`) enforces `MAX_CANDS_PER_S1 = 40` to maintain RAM < 1.5 GB.
11. **Recalculation on Frozen 5,000 Validation Split**:
    - Under the exact validation benchmark blocking: **75.74%**
    - Under the production test inference blocking (with token sort & cap 40): **49.14%**
12. **Authoritative Candidate Recall for Current Validation Benchmark**: **75.74%**.
13. **Arithmetic Reconciliation**: Exactly verified. 13,000 surfaced + 4,164 missed = 17,164 total GT matches.
